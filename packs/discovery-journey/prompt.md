Use the `general-video` skill to make a **Discovery Journey** motion piece — the format that performs on LinkedIn right now: a person's real path to a buying decision, told through the screens they touch.

## Concept

People don't just land on your site. They see a post in their feed, watch a review, ask in a forum thread, compare on search, ask an AI assistant — then decide. Show that journey for *this* product's audience, one beat per screen, ending on the product itself.

Storyboard (adapt to the audience in the brief):

1. **Hook (0–2s):** one big line of kinetic type, e.g. "Nobody finds you on your homepage." (write a sharper one for this product).
2. **4–6 journey beats (~2s each):** stylized, *generic* platform cards — a social feed post, a video review player, a forum thread with upvotes, a search results page, an AI chat answer — each one mentioning this product's real benefit or proof point in that platform's voice. A cursor/finger moves between them; each card flies in, the key phrase highlights, then it hands off to the next with a match-cut or whip. A thin progress line/route map connects the beats like a journey path.
3. **Arrival (3s):** the path lands on the real product UI from the brief's assets — the "aha" screen — with the logo.
4. **Payoff (2–3s):** the share caption as the final line + CTA/URL.

Rules: no real platform logos or trademarks — evoke them with layout, color and UI patterns only. Every quoted line is a plausible paraphrase of a real benefit from the brief, never a fake statistic or a named real person. Avatars are abstract (initials, gradients). Keep text on screen long enough to read (~0.3s/word once settled). Music-driven, cuts on the beat; voiceover: {{voiceover}}.
